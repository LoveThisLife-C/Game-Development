# Production specification migration

Blackout Ridge has been migrated to
`client-input/Blackout_Ridge_Designer_Specification_Production.docx`. The multiplayer shell,
session security, safety controls, chat, reconnect support and visual language
were retained while every act was replaced and validated independently.

## Delivery sequence

| Step | Production act | Status | Playable result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Handover | Implemented | Six-person departure reconciliation with distributed evidence, collective facilitator entry, timeout handling and no correctness reveal or persistent mechanical state |
| 2 | After the Strike | Implemented | Scaled 12-panel console, one repair assignment per player, nine distinct simultaneous one-shot repair games and state-dependent external traffic |
| 3 | Outside Plan | Implemented | Evidence-led function/component/location diagnosis with integrated routing, equipment, plume and visit-order planning |
| 4 | Outside Run | Implemented | Two locked destinations with distinct asymmetric runner/station service games and persistent operational outcomes |
| 5 | Keep the Cabin Breathable | Implemented | Four-minute rotating cabin-control game, performance-based Act 6 time and automatic R-12/Mara reveal |
| 6 | The Lark Shift | Implemented | Timed two-phase searchable archive, period newsreel, R-12 operating rules and final-operator checklist |
| 7 | Get Below | Implemented | Hidden-order transfers, shared refuge purge, solo surface console and survival-time-versus-rescue-time endings |

The production four-to-seven-player limit is now enforced because Act 2 assigns
one distinct repair attempt to every participant.

  repair systems, two credible non-consequential repair systems and three
  systems that survive the strike.
- For X players, consequential systems ranked 1–X and both alternative systems
  begin in FAULT; the crew receives exactly X repair attempts.
- The ranking and later importance are never sent to browser clients.

  surviving systems instead of reproducing the hidden rank order.

## Step 5 acceptance record

- Act 5 is a continuous four-minute survival set piece in Blackout Ridge's one
  occupied cabin, not a hatch-choice form or ventilation simulation.
- Every private terminal shows the same intake, airflow, filter-bank and purge
  controls. Only the named active player can make one change; authority then
  passes to the next present crew role in strict rotation.
- Toxin, CO₂ and filter loading interact through authored state transitions.
  Sealing, opening, recirculating, exhausting and changing filter bank each
  improve one pressure while exposing another; no configuration remains safe.
- External contamination and cabin deterioration continue while players talk.
  Pause and participant-care controls freeze both the clock and air model.
- Working Air Quality provides exact values and trend feedback. Broken Air
  Quality provides qualitative warnings while preserving identical underlying
  rules and full playability.
- Emergency Purge is available only when Upper Ridge service was restored in
  Act 4. It has two finite charges, works best with sealed intake and
  recirculation, and cannot reset the cabin or erase sustained poor play.
- Inbound traffic follows FULL, DEGRADED or OFF communications state and is
  ambient only; no information needed to play or understand the reveal is lost.
- Sustained cabin health, rather than the final setting or move count, converts
  to a guaranteed 180–360 second `ACT6_INVESTIGATION_TIME` window.
- At timeout, the isolation-integrity sweep automatically discovers an unlisted
  sealed protected volume. The old plan names Lower Route and AUXILIARY RELAY
  HOLD — R-12; the dormant feed definitively shows Mara Venn alive inside.
- The reveal is narrative progression for every team. Final contamination,
  control positions, purge use and move count do not persist as new state.
- Server-side authorization, turn rotation, pause, timeout, purge dependency,
  monitoring visibility, fail-forward reveal and end-to-end tests pass.

## Step 6 acceptance record

- Act 6 uses the exact 180–360 second investigation window earned in Act 5 and
  closes automatically; there is no correction form, score or retry.
- The first half exposes short searchable Lark Shift history records, including
  a playable period newsreel with a permanently visible transcript.
- The history establishes an ordinary six-person start, gradual symptoms and
  contradictions, rational checks, escalating fear, deliberate R-12 entry,
  secure isolation, no effective extraction request, a false operational
  picture and recovery after all six had died.
- The initiating cause remains unresolved. The archive explicitly avoids
  blaming the Lark Shift: R-12 protected them from the perceived threat, while
  delayed extraction exhausted their survival window.
- The midpoint unlocks the R-12 technical archive for all terminals. Search and
  opened-document activity remain entirely client-side and are not scored or
  stored in session state.
- The technical cards explain transfer contamination, the advantage of speed,
  finite protected air, one capsule purge per transfer and persistent receiver recovery,
  reduced purge effectiveness before roughly 60 seconds, and separate cabin
  and refuge reserves.
- The exact retained-console sequence is shown consistently: keep Surface
  Contamination Isolation engaged; select AUTHORISED RESPONSE; pull EXTERNAL
  RELEASE TEST; verify ISOLATION HELD and RESPONDER RELEASE ARMED.
- Every player receives the same final checklist: DISTRESS → EXTRACTION MESSAGE
  → RELEASE CONFIGURATION. The archive warns that descent order will be hidden
  and microphones below will be muted.
- The archive identifies Mara's Act 3 transmission as an obsolete internal R-12
  circuit and repeats that OPERATIONAL describes the station/relay, not people.
- Broken Air Quality changes only deadline precision, replacing the countdown
  with qualitative urgency bands. It never hides records or changes duration.
- FULL, DEGRADED or OFF inbound traffic supplies ambience only; every mandatory
  fact remains in the local archive.
- Resolution stores only the investigation-window duration and creates no new
  persistent mechanical state. The three finale tasks are learned, not executed.
- Automated authorization, phase-unlock, privacy, fail-forward, asset and
  end-to-end tests pass.

## Step 7 acceptance record

- Act 7 begins automatically from the common critical cabin transition. Mara
  remains inside R-12; the reversal is that she is already where the surface
  crew now needs to go.
- The server creates a random hidden descent order. Clients never receive the
  sequence or an assigned position; they see only active, waiting or below.
- Exactly one player receives the four-action physical transfer at a time.
  Elapsed time and mistakes add shared refuge contamination. A 60-second
  timeout always completes the transfer with the maximum penalty.
- R-12 starts clean and Mara adds no initial penalty. Every crew transfer adds
  contamination; player count is included later in protected-air endurance.
- Players below lose crew chat and cannot act on surface controls or coach
  players above. Their terminal shows only refuge condition and, when
  available, the five private investigation hotspots.
- Upper Ridge RESTORED provides three shared refuge purge cycles; otherwise
  none exist. Purge consumes protected-air reserve and never restores perfect
  air. Repeating before approximately 60 seconds remains valid but removes less.
- The purge control locks briefly during execution. No recharge countdown or
  optimum-time hint is shown.
- The final random player receives a 30-second solo dashboard using exactly the
  Act 6 terminology: DISTRESS, extraction message, contamination isolation,
  AUTHORISED RESPONSE and EXTERNAL RELEASE TEST.
- Surface submit or timeout always begins the final transfer; nobody is left or
  sacrificed above.
- The facilitator classifies the already-committed extraction text as
  EFFECTIVE, PARTIAL or INEFFECTIVE using an explicit content-only rubric.
- Status Beacon, Human Outbound plus the restored external path, and retained
  release configuration independently determine effective station status,
  message quality and responder access.
- Any unused purge cycles are applied at full recovered effectiveness after
  seal, including their protected-air cost.
- The explicit balance asset converts final air plus occupancy into
  `R12_SURVIVAL_TIME` and external outcomes into `RESCUE_TIME`.
- Their margin selects exactly one production ending band: Clean Rescue, Close
  / Severe Rescue, Rescue With Casualties or Lark Shift Echo.
- The facilitator previews and confirms the calculated ending before debrief.
- Automated tests cover authorization, privacy, isolation, timeout,
  contamination, purge recovery, independent external outcomes, ending bands
  and complete fail-forward play.

## Decisions to preserve during later steps

- Human problem first; technical terminology follows the human situation.
- Each act uses a different core interaction.
- Earlier operational choices affect later capability, information, time or
  rescue delay without terminating play.
- On-screen information is not a substitute for player communication and
  understanding.
- `OPERATIONAL / NO ACTIVE DISTRESS` cannot simply be switched off.
- Difficulty must come from communication and application, not specialist
  knowledge, arbitrary codes or long reading.
